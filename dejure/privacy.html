Privacy Real Talk

We make our apps based on the principle of data minimization, just like Apple, the European Union, and many others.

We know there is a balance between running a service, and overzealously collecting information, which is why we’ve written the following list to clearly delineate where we draw the line.

  • Our intention is to embed privacy by design in the app.
  • There is no code from Facebook (Meta), Google, nor TikTok in the app.
  • Personally identifiable information is not required to use the app.
  • If you choose to enter any personally identifiable information, we make our best effort to store it securely.
  • We do not sell your data (or any data for that matter) and have no intention to.
