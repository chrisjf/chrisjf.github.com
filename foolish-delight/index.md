# Foolish Delight



## [First Things First](https://christophe.ca/foolish-delight/first-things-first/)

Hello!

Even though it's a chilly autumn day in Montréal, I've got a little cup of homemade coconut-cardamom ice cream in front of me. My name is Chris, creator of mobile apps, the guy who definitely licked the whisk before the custard cooled, and who’s clearly in deep denial about the coming winter.

Why am I starting this little corner of the internet? I want a place to call my own. An unhurried space, an open kitchen with simple ingredients, and a little bit of cream and spice.

Making ice cream from scratch takes patience: dreaming up doubtful pairings, letting the flavours mingle, and occasionally burning the batch only to start all over again. Churning out words by hand in the age of artificial intelligence may seem a bit quaint. But that foolishness is kind of the point. So grab something sweet and let's be foolish together.
